# lvl_drop_test: the onboarding level

The first contract a player meets (the drop-and-extract trial, decision
0026): it teaches the line by its layout, with no text. South to north,
each lesson a little higher and further than the last, and every lesson
with ground the horde cannot easily reach under it.

| Lesson | Where | What teaches it |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | The landing (0, 380) | Three 26 m columns 25-60 m ahead (`FirstHook*`): close, low, impossible to miss. |
| 2. Swing | The company road | A raised embankment 10 m up, reached only by its ramps; 36 m pylons 77 m apart beside it (`RoadPylon*`). A missed swing lands on the road, not in the horde. |
| 3. Go high | The jar court | Two 95 m towers flank the 40 m jar (`CourtTower*`) and a ring of columns: zip up, drop onto the lid (the short way in). |
| 4. Graduate | The routes out | Every pickup reached through the air, hops 30-110 m (the grapple is 140 m), up to the level's tops: the Butte camp's tower (123 m), the west mesa's lookout (55 m mesa + 95 m), the summit on the north-east mesa (45 m + 122 m), Drill Rig 1 (122 m). |

Walking is the slow way, never the stuck way: ramps climb the Butte (by a
land bridge from the road), both mesas, and the road; their cliffs are walls
the horde cannot climb (slopes over 42 deg).

The story is told by what stands there, not by signs: an evacuated survey
camp with its comms tower caught falling, the previous contractor's pod
nose-down at the north-east mesa, a drill rig waiting for the site to be
cleared.

Built by hand through the editor bridge (rule 8, decision 0044). Check a
change against the grapple's reach from the nodes' positions, and look at it
from where a player stands.
