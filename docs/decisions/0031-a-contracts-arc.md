# 0031 Proposed: land as you like, and earn drops within the contract

*18 October 2026. A proposal, not yet built or played. Builds on 0010, 0012,
0019 and 0026.*

## Context

0026 took the bike away because it solved everything, and left open where it
comes back ("a call-in, an upgrade, a boss"). The pitch's company packages
arrive on their own inside a mission; persistent unlocks across missions
follow the Deep Rock Galactic and Helldivers model. The arsenal is about nine
weapons from the prototype, tuned piecemeal.

## Decision

- **Two ways to start, both valid.** The player picks a landing zone, then
  either scouts in first on the light hoverbike or drops straight into the
  fight. These are two kinds of player, not a right choice and a trap; neither
  is balanced to punish the other.
- **Drops within a contract: random, automatic, one-off.** During a contract
  the company sends allied mechs, vehicles and random weapon drops, with no
  choice at pickup. They last the contract and never touch long-term
  progression. They build up, with the hoverbike and its laser as the
  late-contract reward.
- **Persistent progression stays as it is.**
- **Every current weapon is reshaped.** Which stay, and what each becomes, is
  its own decision, still to be written.
- **Both lasers exist.** The bike's beam is the one you hold down: RimWorld's
  orbital beam at bike scale. The personal one is a shoulder cannon, on a
  cooldown or as a drop.

## Costs and open ends

- Scouting needs the light bike back on the planet, and 0026's trial
  has no bike. This waits for the trial's answers.
- What a drop's odds and order are, and how often the company sends one, are
  data, not yet numbers.
- Whether the scout bike and the reward bike are one machine with its weapons
  locked, or two, is not decided.
- Drops add mechs and vehicles to a fight already at 25,000: `mechs` and
  `mechsab` measure what allies cost alongside the horde.
