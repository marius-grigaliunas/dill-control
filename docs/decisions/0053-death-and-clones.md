# 0053 Death is cloning: three clones a contract, failure pays nothing, nothing is lost

*Decided with the slice, 22-23 October 2026 (the design brief gives this rule no
date of its own); built in `lvl_tutorial` (`CloneBank`, 0055). Refines the earlier clone trial and the pitch's "after the
third the company stops bringing you back". Builds on 0051.*

## Context

Death was settled early: cloning, three tries. What losing the third costs was
an open question ("debt, gear or the contract"). Taking a favourite weapon away
over one mistake makes a player quit.

## Decision

- **Three clones a contract**, the first body included. Each comes down in a pod
  at the landing site (0054).
- **Clones are chances to get paid, not a way to keep gear.** Death costs
  nothing the player carries: **no gear or specials are lost.**
- **Losing the third fails the contract.** The player wakes on the ship unpaid,
  **keeps every upgrade**, and can replay that contract or any unlocked one.
- **The punishment is money only.** A failed contract pays nothing, and the
  invoice charges clone fees, ammo and damage against the fee (0052's shop spends
  what is left).
- Losing gear or specials on death was **rejected**: a player who loses a
  favourite weapon to one mistake rage-quits.
- The first death in contract 1 teaches clones, with the lab flashes of 0054.

## Tie to the parked debt idea

The money-based punishment is where the parked story (`docs/ideas/story.md`)
attaches: once built, deaths and failures also add to a debt, with a cap so no
player is ever buried. That is not decided and not in the slice; the invoice
shows no debt balance until it is.

## Costs and open ends

- A player with no money pressure has little reason to avoid dying beyond the
  clone count; the invoice's weight depends on how much the shop costs.
- A clone is a new player scene made while a "printing" screen covers it; its
  frame cost is unmeasured.
- How a failed contract reads in the cabin (the same arrival, an invoice stamped
  with a different word) is for the cabin's design.
